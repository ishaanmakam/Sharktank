Fueling at the airport

  • Making the fuel: solar panels and electrolysis (splitting water) right at the airport
  • Storing it: insulated tanks with safe distances from everything else
  • Small airports: swappable fuel pods by truck, first flown in Moses Lake in 2023
  • Washington is ready: the DOE picked the Pacific Northwest Hydrogen Hub for up to $1 billion
  • More than 220 airports have joined Airbus's hydrogen airport study
