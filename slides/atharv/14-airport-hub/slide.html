Civil · the ground game

No plane flies without an airport that makes fuel.

We design the hydrogen hub: solar and electrolysis on airport land, a liquefier, an insulated tank farm with safe setbacks, and pipes to the gate. Smaller airports get swappable fuel pods by truck, an idea first flown at Moses Lake in 2023.

Washington is ready for it. The DOE picked the Pacific Northwest Hydrogen Hub for up to $1B, and more than 220 airports have joined Airbus's hydrogen hub study.

Why civil: siting, foundations, water supply, permits and safety zones are what turn a hydrogen idea into concrete on the ground.
