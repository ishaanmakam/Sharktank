What we designed around

Eight things a hydrogen plane can't get wrong.

SafetyLeaks, fire and crashes. Hydrogen escapes fast and burns invisibly, so detection and venting come first.
AffordabilityGreen hydrogen is still expensive, so every kilogram of weight and drag we cut matters.
EfficiencyMore energy per kilogram only helps if the tank and powertrain don't eat it back.
ClimateZero CO₂ in flight, but water vapor and contrails still warm the planet.
NoiseRegional airports sit next to neighborhoods, so quieter props are part of the deal.
End of lifeComposites that end up in landfills defeat the point. Ours have to come apart and be reused.
AccessSmall cities, not just hubs. It has to work at airports that don't have a hydrogen plant yet.
InfrastructureThe plane is useless without the fuel, the storage and the people to run it on the ground.
