What we thought about

  • Safety: hydrogen leaks fast and burns almost invisibly
  • Cost: green hydrogen is still expensive, so every bit of drag matters
  • Efficiency: the tank can't eat up the weight we save
  • Climate: no CO₂, but contrails still count
  • Noise: small airports are right next to neighborhoods
  • End of life: the plane should be recyclable
  • Access: it has to work at small airports too
  • Infrastructure: no fuel at the airport, no flight
