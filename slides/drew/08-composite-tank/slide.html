Composite structures · the tank

A fuel tank colder than anywhere on Earth.

NASA and Boeing tested a 5.5 m all-composite cryogenic tank that came out 30% lighter and at least 25% cheaper. At −253 °C, metals get brittle, hydrogen sneaks through tiny cracks, and layers shrink at different rates. That's a materials problem first.
