A skin that heals itself

  • Tiny channels inside the carbon fiber work like blood vessels
  • When a crack opens them, two liquids mix and harden
  • It heals almost 100%, and can do it more than once
  • Inside the cabin we use flax fiber panels (a plant) instead of some fiberglass
