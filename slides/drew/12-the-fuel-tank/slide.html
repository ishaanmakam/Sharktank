The fuel tank

  • Made of carbon fiber composite instead of metal
  • NASA and Boeing tested a 5.5 m composite tank that was:
    • 30% lighter
    • At least 25% cheaper
  • Problems at −253 °C:
    • Metal gets brittle
    • Hydrogen leaks through tiny cracks
    • Layers shrink at different rates

CompositesWorld, "NASA/Boeing composite cryotank passes structural load testing" (2014)
