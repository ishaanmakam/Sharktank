Keeping it cold

The hydrogen has to warm up by about 300 °C before the fuel cells can use it, and the fuel cells make heat we need to get rid of anyway.

So we use one to fix the other.

This is the mechanical engineer's job: heat exchangers, pumps, valves and the tank mounts.
