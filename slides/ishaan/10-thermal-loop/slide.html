Mechanical · thermal management

The cold is a feature, not a problem.

The hydrogen has to warm up by about 300 °C before the fuel cells can use it, and the fuel cells make heat we have to dump. So we let one fix the other, and the water that comes out goes straight to the cabin tap.

Why mechanical: heat exchangers, pumps, valves and the tank's mounts all live where thermodynamics meets hardware. One cryogenic leak decides whether this plane ever flies.
