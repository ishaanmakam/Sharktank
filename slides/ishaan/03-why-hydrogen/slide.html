Why hydrogen?

  • Batteries: way too heavy for an airliner
  • Liquid hydrogen: almost 3x the energy of jet fuel per kilogram, and the only exhaust is water
  • The catch:
    • It takes up about 4x the space
    • It has to stay at −253 °C
