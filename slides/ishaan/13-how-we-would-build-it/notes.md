[Ishaan] We also thought about how to build it. Thermoplastic composites can be melted down and reshaped, so scrap gets reused instead of thrown away. Europe already built a test fuselage by welding the parts together, and it came out about a tonne lighter and up to 40% faster to make. The robots that do this are made in Mukilteo, and UW's Boeing Advanced Research Center is already working on it.
