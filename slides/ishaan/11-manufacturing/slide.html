Sustainable manufacturing

Welded, not riveted. Melted down, not dumped.

Europe's thermoplastic fuselage demonstrator welds its stiffeners on instead of drilling thousands of holes. It came out about a tonne lighter than aluminum and skips the autoclave, cutting production time by up to 40%. Thermoplastics also re-melt, so scrap and old parts become new ones.

We'd build it next door. Robots like this fiber-placement head are made in Mukilteo, and UW's Boeing Advanced Research Center is already funded to automate thermoplastic parts.
