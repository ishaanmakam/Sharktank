What's the problem?

Planes are one of the hardest things to make clean. You can't just plug an airliner into a wall like a car.

  • Flying makes about 2.5% of the world's CO₂
  • Once you count contrails, it's closer to 4% of global warming so far
  • And air travel keeps growing every year
