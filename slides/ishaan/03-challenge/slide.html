The challenge

Hydrogen is the lightest fuel and the hardest to carry.

Batteries are far too heavy for an airliner. Liquid hydrogen packs almost three times the energy of jet fuel per kilogram and burns into water, but it takes four times the space and has to stay at −253 °C.

So the real question isn't the fuel. It's the whole airplane, built around it.
