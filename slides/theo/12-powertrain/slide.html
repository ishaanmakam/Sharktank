Electrical · the powertrain

Fuel cells, superconducting wires, quiet props.

Fuel cells turn hydrogen into electricity and water, with no flame. Then we use the −253 °C fuel one more time: it chills the cables and motors until they superconduct. Airbus expects that trick to at least halve the weight and electrical losses of the powertrain.
