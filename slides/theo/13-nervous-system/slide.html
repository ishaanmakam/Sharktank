A plane with a nervous system that dodges clouds.

Fiber-optic sensors woven into the composite feel strain, cold spots and leaks everywhere at once, and feed a digital twin of the airframe.

Hydrogen puts out about 2.6× more water vapor per unit of energy, but it may cut contrail ice crystals by 80 to 90%. Our flight software reads humidity forecasts and nudges the route around the air where contrails would linger.

Why ECE: power electronics, sensors, controls and code are what turn a tank of hydrogen into a plane that flies itself safely.
