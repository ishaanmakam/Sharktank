Bio-inspired aerodynamics · 1

A nose from a kingfisher, a skin from a shark.

Japan reshaped its 500 Series bullet train around a kingfisher's beak and it ran about 10% faster on 15% less energy. Shark-skin riblet film already flies on 777s and cuts fuel burn by about 1%, and that adds up fast across a whole fleet.
