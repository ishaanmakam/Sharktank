Parts of Halcyon

  • Nose: shaped like a kingfisher's beak
  • Skin: shark-skin film to cut drag
  • Wings: whale-fin bumps and albatross wingtips
  • Propellers: owl-feather edges for less noise
  • Fuel tank: carbon fiber, holds the liquid hydrogen
  • Fuel cells: turn hydrogen into electricity
  • Water tank: stores the water the fuel cells make
